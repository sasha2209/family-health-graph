Practice clinic for staff training

A clinic filled with 14 made-up families that between them cover every care program and referral status, so staff can rehearse the worklist, a patient summary and referrals before real patients arrive. Staff join it in the clinic portal with the staff code. Its patients are not counted in the pilot report, real patients can't join it, and its referrals are never sent to partners. Delete it after training.
