# Security

Family Health Graph holds family health records, so we take reports seriously.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please email the security contact configured for the deployment (`FHG_GRIEVANCE_OFFICER_EMAIL` doubles as the
privacy and security contact until a dedicated address is set), with steps to reproduce. Don't open a public issue,
and don't access, change or delete other people's data while testing; use accounts you created.

We aim to acknowledge reports within 3 working days and to tell you what we're doing about them.
